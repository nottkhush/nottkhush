### 🎙️ Meet AI &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Live](https://meet-application-eight.vercel.app/) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [GitHub](https://github.com/nottkhush/meet-application)

> *What if your job interview prep coach, sales trainer, and language tutor were all available 24/7 and never judged you?*

That's Meet AI. Real-time voice conversations with custom AI agents - you build the agent, you set the personality, you pick the use case. After every call it auto-generates a summary, transcript, and recording so you actually remember what happened.
